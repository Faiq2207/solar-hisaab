How much electricity does it make?

With about 5 hours of strong sun a day in Lahore, 6 panels (3.5kW) make roughly 14 units a day, or about 411 units a month. That’s enough to run fans, lights, a fridge, TV and a small water motor during the day, or one 1 ton inverter AC for a few hours.
