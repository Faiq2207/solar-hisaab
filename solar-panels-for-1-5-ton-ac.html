Running an AC at night

Panels only make power in daylight. To run a 1.5 ton inverter AC for 4 hours at night you need about 4 units of battery storage, which is roughly one 48V 100Ah lithium battery (Rs 230,000) plus a hybrid inverter.

Inverter AC or non-inverter on solar?

An inverter AC uses about 40% less power once the room is cool, so it needs fewer panels and a smaller inverter. If you’re buying a new AC for a solar home, an inverter AC almost always pays for itself.

Common questions

How many solar panels do I need for a 1.5 ton inverter AC?

About 4 panels of 585W to run a 1.5 ton inverter AC for 8 hours a day, not counting your other appliances.

Can I run an AC on solar without batteries?

Yes, during the day. With a hybrid or on-grid inverter, the AC runs on solar while the sun is up and switches to the grid or batteries after sunset.

What size inverter do I need for a 1.5 ton AC?

A 6kW hybrid inverter is the common choice for a home with one 1.5 ton AC plus normal appliances.
