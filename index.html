Common questions

How many solar panels do I need for a 1.5 ton inverter AC?

A 1.5 ton inverter AC running 8 hours uses roughly 8 units a day. That needs about 2 kW of panels, which is 3 to 4 panels of 585 watts, on top of what the rest of your home uses. Add your AC above to see your exact number.

Is solar still worth it after net billing?

Under the 2026 rules, extra power you send to the grid is bought at a much lower rate, around Rs 11 per unit. Solar still saves the most when you use the power yourself, during the day or through batteries, because every unit you don’t buy saves you your full bill rate.

Do I need batteries?

Without batteries, solar only runs your home during the day and switches off in a power cut. Batteries let you run lights, fans and the fridge at night and during load-shedding, but they add a lot to the cost.

How accurate is this calculator?

It gives a good starting estimate using average sunshine for Lahore and typical appliance power. Real numbers depend on your roof, brand choice and how you use your appliances, so use it to compare quotes, not replace them.
